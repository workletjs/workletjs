Interaction component that allows the user to zoom the map by double tap/click then drag up/down
with one finger/left mouse.

## Examples

### Double click, Drag and Zoom

A single interaction to zoom in/out by double clicking and dragging. On the second click, hold the
mouse left button down and drag up or down to zoom in or out. Works also with touch devices.
